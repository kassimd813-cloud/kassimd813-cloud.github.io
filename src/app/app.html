§1 — introduction

An educator shaped by classrooms across Cape Town.

Olutomi Adedotun Kassim has taught in Western Cape schools since 2010, beginning at Simon Estes Music High School in Wynberg and moving through Focus College, where he served as Head of Mathematics (GET) and Mathematical Literacy, and Sinenjongo High School as a WCED educator.

Today he is the GET Mathematics Coordinator at Bothasig High School. Alongside school teaching, he has directed academic affairs at the Rainbow Dream Trust in Hout Bay since 2011 and spent nearly a decade as a tutor and lesson coordinator with ASSET, consolidating Grade 8 and 9 Mathematics.

He holds a PGCE in Mathematics and Life Sciences from MANCOSA and completed a short course in Mathematics at Stellenbosch University in 2024. He is also an accredited assessor and moderator.
